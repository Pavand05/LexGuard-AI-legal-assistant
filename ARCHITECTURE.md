# LexGuard-MA: Multi-Agent Legal Document Intelligence & Contract Analysis Platform Architecture

## Executive Overview
**LexGuard-MA** transforms traditional single-prompt legal AI pipelines into a specialized, collaborative multi-agent network designed for Indian legal frameworks (Indian Contract Act 1872, DPDP Act 2023, Information Technology Act 2000, Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996) and general commercial contract analysis.

### Topologies
1. **Parallel Multi-Agent (Production Default)**:
   - Independent analytical agents execute concurrently via thread pools (`ThreadPoolExecutor`), achieving sub-200ms latency without bottlenecking downstream consensus.
2. **Sequential Multi-Agent**:
   - Step-by-step linear execution where each agent directly consumes enriched context and metadata generated by preceding agents.
3. **Debate / Critic Mode**:
   - Risk & Compliance findings are challenged and verified by the Citation Critic Agent, followed by an adjudicating Reviewer Agent.
4. **Single-Agent Baseline (Benchmark Mode)**:
   - Monolithic prompt evaluation baseline used for empirical research and comparative ablation.

## 3. Mathematical Risk Scoring & Contract Health Formulas

### Multi-Dimensional Risk Score
$$\text{Risk}_{\text{Total}} = \sum_{d \in D} w_d \cdot S_d$$
Where dimensions $D = \{\text{Legal}: 0.25, \text{Financial}: 0.20, \text{Compliance}: 0.20, \text{Privacy}: 0.15, \text{Operational}: 0.10, \text{IP}: 0.10\}$.

### Contract Health Score (0–100)
$$\text{Health Score} = 100 - \min(40, 12 \cdot N_{\text{high}}) - \min(25, 8 \cdot N_{\text{comp}}) - \min(15, 5 \cdot N_{\text{cit\_unv}}) - \min(20, 6 \cdot N_{\text{missing}})$$

## 4. Storage & Observability Architecture
- **AgentRun**: Captures run ID, workflow type, latency in ms, health score, and JSON telemetry traces.
- **AgentFinding**: Normalizes structured findings across all 13 agents for queryable analytics.
- **ContractObligation**: Extracts trackable, event-based obligations with deadline management.
- **EvaluationRun**: Stores empirical benchmark metrics across system configurations.
